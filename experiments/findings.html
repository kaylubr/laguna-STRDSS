What deserves a later look

The nearby class mix of other listings (EXP-A05) and 3 km road density (EXP-E03) raised grouped-CV macro F1 the most. Stacking every extra column (EXP-COMBINED-01) also raised leave-one-municipality-out mean F1 from 0.292 to 0.345. Nested hyperparameter search did not beat the fixed production settings. Keep the official map on the five-feature forest until those two groups are reviewed one at a time.
