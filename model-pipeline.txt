LAGUNA SHORT-TERM RENTAL PERFORMANCE
Describes the model that is actually running after scripts/run_rural.py.
The map class is the random forest's predicted historical performance class.
It is not an entropy-weighted score and not a five-class Fisher-Jenks cut.

The question is whether surrounding Airbnb conditions and landscape places
are associated with Low, Moderate, or High historical Airbnb performance.
It is not a generic investment ranking.

On the latest municipality holdout the forest's macro F1 was 0.330, above
the majority-class baseline of 0.242 and below the stratified baseline of
0.356. One-vs-rest ROC-AUC was 0.486, below 0.50. Do not describe the map
as evidence that the available features generalize to new municipalities.

================================================================================
1. TARGET
================================================================================

Only cells with at least one active listing are labeled. 1,476 cells with
no listing stay unlabeled. They are not called Low.

For each labeled cell, min-max normalize its own mean trailing-twelve-month
revenue and its own mean occupancy across the 288 labeled cells:

  normalized = (value - minimum) / (maximum - minimum)

  historical performance = 0.5 * normalized revenue + 0.5 * normalized occupancy

That score is the training target only. It is not applied after prediction.

  score <= 25th percentile (0.065)  -> Low        72 cells, 25%
  between the cutoffs               -> Moderate  144 cells, 50%
  score >= 75th percentile (0.230)  -> High       72 cells, 25%

A cell exactly on the 25th percentile is Low. A cell exactly on the 75th
percentile is High. The cell's own revenue and occupancy are not features.

================================================================================
2. FEATURES
================================================================================

Radius: 5 km. Haversine from the cell centroid.

Airbnb market, excluding listings inside the cell:
  surrounding_mean_revenue
  surrounding_mean_occupancy
  surrounding_listing_count
    Zero count means no other active listing was inside the radius.
    The two means stay missing in that case. They are not filled with zero.
    A mean of zero occurs only when surrounding listings exist and average zero.
    The forest accepts the missing values directly.

Landscape places, from data/poi_laguna.json:
  172 curated places: 63 falls, 18 mountains, 23 lakes and ponds,
  68 rivers and landscape. Counted together. Not OpenStreetMap shops,
  restaurants, or transport. A rating field, if present, is ignored.
  listed_places_within_radius
  distance_to_listed_tourist_place

Last run: 1,764 cells scored. 122 have no surrounding listing.
0 scored cells had a non-missing feature outside the training min/max.
Missing means are not counted as outside that range.

================================================================================
3. MODEL AND VALIDATION
================================================================================

RandomForestClassifier
  n_estimators 200, max_depth 10, max_features sqrt,
  min_samples_leaf 1, min_samples_split 2,
  class_weight balanced, random_state 42.
These settings are config.CLASSIFIER_PARAMS. They are not chosen by
searching the test municipalities.

Split: StratifiedGroupKFold by municipality, all 5 folds, shuffle, random_state 42.
A municipality is entirely train or test. Each labeled cell is in one test fold.
The map model is refit on all 288 labeled cells with the same settings.
Experiment scores do not replace this specification.

================================================================================
5. ROAD DISTANCE, NOT IN THE MAP MODEL
================================================================================

data/processed/grid_road_distance.parquet is the straight-line distance from
each of the 1,764 cell centroids to the nearest mapped road in
data/laguna_roads.geojson. That file is an OpenStreetMap/Geofabrik extract.
It is not DPWH data. Every mapped road is eligible, including local roads.
Distances are metres in EPSG:32651, also stored in kilometres.
Latest distances: minimum 0.08 m, median 146 m, mean 307 m, maximum 3.64 km.

A five-fold comparison that adds this distance to the five current features
raised mean macro F1 by 0.041 and mean ROC-AUC by 0.013. The gain was not on
every held-out municipality, so the feature was not added to the map model.
See docs/road-accessibility.md.
